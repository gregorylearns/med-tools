    Paste Patient Entries

    You can optionally declare global variables (e.g. Department, Date, Department Head, Instruments Needed) at the very top. Then enter each patient's details as numbered entries separated by blank lines.

    Bug: Live Preview does not work on Batch OR PROPOSAL MAKER

    Example:

     Department: SURGERY
 Date: 08/20/2026
 Department-Head: DR. SOMEONE
 Instruments: MAJOR LAPAROTOMY SET

 Sex: M
 Impression: ACUTE UNCOMPLICATED APPENDICITIS

 Surgery-date: 08/21/2026
 Est-time: 1 HOUR
