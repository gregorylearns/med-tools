Forms Generator

Paste Patient Entries

You can optionally declare global variables (e.g. Department, Date, Department Head, Instruments Needed) at the very top. Then enter each patient's details as numbered entries separated by blank lines.

Bug: Live Preview does not work on Batch OR PROPOSAL MAKER

Example:

Department: SURGERY
Date: 08/20/2026
Department-Head: DR. SOMEONE
Instruments: MAJOR LAPAROTOMY SET

1. Name: Juan Dela Cruz
Age: 45
Sex: M
Impression: ACUTE UNCOMPLICATED APPENDICITIS
Proposed Operation: LAPAROTOMY APPENDECTOMY
Surgeon: DR. IBONALO, LEO MARC
Asst-surgeon: DR. CARREON, ARBIE JOHN
Anes: AROD
Surgery-date: 08/21/2026
Est-time: 1 HOUR
Floor: SP3
Position: SUPINE


2. Name: DALISAY CARDITA
Age: 28
Sex: F
Impression: CHOLELITHIASIS
Proposed Operation: LAPAROSCOPIC CHOLECYSTECTOMY, POSSIBLE OPEN
Surgeon: DR. PAJANTOY, A LIESON
Asst-surgeon: DR. JOSOL, ICE
Anes: AROD
Surgery-date: 08/21/2026

Tip: press the Tab key after each input field to move to the next field
