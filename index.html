Paste Patient Entries

Enter each patient's details as numbered entries. Separate entries with blank lines.

Example:

1. Name: Juan Dela Cruz
Age: 45
Sex: M
Impression: Fracture of left radius with displacement
Proposed Operation: Open reduction internal fixation

2. Name: Maria Santos
Age: 28
Sex: F
Impression: Acute appendicitis
Proposed Operation: Laparoscopic appendectomy
Surgeon: Dr. Reyes

Tip: press the Tab key after each input field to move to the next field
